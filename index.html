How it works

1. Detect: finds the smallest rectangle containing every non-transparent pixel.

2. Crop: removes transparent pixels around the outside only.

3. Resize: scales proportionally so neither dimension exceeds your selected maximum.

The image contains no visible pixels, so there is nothing to crop.

Fit artwork preserves the trimmed image proportions. Square canvas makes both output dimensions exactly the selected size, with optional top, centre, or bottom alignment. Rotation automatically scales the artwork down when necessary so no part is clipped.
